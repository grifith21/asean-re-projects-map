# ASEAN Renewable Energy Projects Map

Interactive map of renewable energy units and phases in the 11 ASEAN
countries, by project stage, technology and country.

## Data
Source: Global Energy Monitor, Global Integrated Power Tracker,
[month year] release. https://globalenergymonitor.org/projects/global-integrated-power-tracker
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
Changes made: records filtered to six renewable technologies in ASEAN
countries and reformatted for display. [Number] manually researched
entries are not from GEM and are not plotted.

## Limitations
- Each symbol is a unit or phase, not a whole project.
- About 46% of locations are approximate, mostly in announced,
  pre-construction, shelved and cancelled stages.
- Coverage follows GEM's capacity thresholds; smaller facilities may be absent.
- The map shows project stage only. It does not show disputes or the
  reasons projects were shelved or cancelled.
